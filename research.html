Connection & scope

Configure an application key and authenticated session in the server environment; see docs/BETFAIR.md in the project. Credentials stay on the server. With no connection, Betfair shows no prices.

Fixture links identify corresponding events and runners. Settlement equivalence remains unverified; Betfair is excluded from prediction-market alerts, fee estimates and PnL. Native odds and stakes remain separate from cents and contract quantities. This iteration adds no trading or streaming.
