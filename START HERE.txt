PREDICTION MARKET TERMINAL

1. Open this folder in VS Code or Terminal.
2. Start the browser terminal: python3 server.py
3. Keep it running and use http://127.0.0.1:8765/CricketMatchingReport.html
4. Quotes refresh automatically every 15 seconds. Reload or click Refresh prices
   to request another refresh. The table updates without reopening the page.
   First-time setup needs one full scan: python3 run.py

Use --interval=30 for a slower cycle or --interval=0 for manual refresh.

For a standalone CLI price refresh:
python3 run.py --snapshot-dir=output/data --refresh-quotes
The direct output/CricketMatchingReport.html file is a snapshot export; use the
HTTP terminal above for browser-triggered refreshes. Ctrl+C stops the service.

The Sports and Esports category tabs separate the market universes. Esports
starts with CS2 and League of Legends; Finance and Tech are future categories.

Gross cents - Fees cents = Net cents per paired contract. Est. PnL dollars is
the total estimated profit at the actual displayed size. The default request
is 100 contracts, capped to confirmed top depth; both taker fees are rounded
for that order size. After fees + keeps positive estimates. A 2c or 3c gross
cross can disappear after fees. Click an event for the full fee breakdown.

Min top liquidity defaults to $100 of paired top notional on each selected
leg. This is available depth, not market volume. Unknown depth is hidden until
you explicitly include it. Thin markets are probed again after 60 seconds.
Star useful rows, then open Watchlist to narrow the service's quote requests.
The service shares these settings across its browser tabs.

Bid/ask quantities appear beside their prices. Click column headings to sort,
use league/search filters, or enable Extra columns. Full names, settlement
payouts and fee/depth/capital assumptions are in details and docs/FEES.md.

Each run fetches current Kalshi and Polymarket International match winners for
cricket, soccer, basketball, American football, hockey, baseball, tennis, MMA,
Counter-Strike 2 and League of Legends.
Basketball and American football include women's/college leagues where available.
To select sports, pass for example:
python3 run.py --sports=football,hockey,baseball --no-open
python3 run.py --sports=esports --no-open
For a subset of the 17 supported soccer competitions, run:
python3 run.py --sports=soccer --soccer-leagues=epl,ucl,uel --no-open
The report shows its collection timestamp, sport/venue coverage, and advisories.

The local Python service keeps the market universe in memory; routine quote
refreshes do not rebuild HTML or archive snapshots. Run a full scan periodically
to discover newly opened markets. HTML/JavaScript remains the browser UI, with
Python owning data collection and the future exchange socket connections.

The scanner uses public read-only APIs and does not place orders. The
incremental quote engine and subscription manifests prepare for WebSocket
integration; venue sockets are not connected yet. See docs/FEEDS.md.
